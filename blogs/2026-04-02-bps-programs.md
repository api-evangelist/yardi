<p>Across the U.S. and Canada, an increasing number of jurisdictions are adopting Building Performance Standards (BPS) — regulations that require building owners to meet defined targets for energy efficiency, water usage and greenhouse gas emissions. In parallel, many cities and states have introduced Audit, Tune-Up and Retro-commissioning policies, which mandate technical upgrades for underperforming buildings.</p>



<p></p>



<p>These programs vary significantly in terms of scope, applicable building types and reporting requirements, making compliance a complex and evolving challenge for many organizations.</p>



<p></p>



<p>The table below provides a brief overview of BPS programs that have been adopted or are currently in development, along with key details for each program.</p>
