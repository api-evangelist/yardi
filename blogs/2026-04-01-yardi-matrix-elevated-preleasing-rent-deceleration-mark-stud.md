<p><strong>SANTA BARBARA, Calif., April 1, 2026 –</strong> Estimated preleasing for the Yardi® 200 schools in February 2026 exceeded the initial estimates from the previous two Februarys even as rent growth continues to decelerate, according to new data from <a href="http://clicks.yardi.com/t?r=3303&amp;c=3840064&amp;l=408860&amp;ctl=4BF6C01:BF771BA8D113713ACA1F6AC6428E05631A0C639E274EDC65&amp;" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Yardi<sup>®</sup> Matrix</a>.</p>



<p>Preleasing reached 58.6% in February, tracking ahead of final February figures in previous years. But while the average per-bed rent rose year-over-year that month, the 0.4% growth represents a decline from January’s 0.6% growth and was well below the 3.5% growth recorded in February 2025.</p>



<p>“Despite solid enrollment growth of 1.8% in fall 2025 and relatively modest supply additions, early indicators for the 2026-2027 academic year point to a normalization in performance following years of record growth,” according to a new report from Yardi Matrix.</p>



<p>Other key factors include competition from a soft multifamily sector, particularly in urban markets where some new multifamily projects have targeted student residents while in lease-up.</p>
