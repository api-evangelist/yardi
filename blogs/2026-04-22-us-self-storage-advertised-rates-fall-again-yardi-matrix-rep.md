<p><strong>SANTA BARBARA, Calif., April 22, 2026 –</strong> Subdued advertised rate growth in U.S. self storage reflects ongoing demand softness across markets and unit types, according to new data from <a href="https://www.yardimatrix.com/publications/download/file/8538-MatrixSelfStorageNationalReport-April2026?signup=false">Yardi® Matrix</a>.</p>



<p>National advertised rates decreased 2% in March 2026, more than the 1.2% and 0.4% declines in February and January, respectively. Almost all of the Yardi Matrix top 30 metros had lower year-over-year rate growth in March compared to February, and all top metros experienced negative annual growth. Moreover, the decline in advertised rates spans non-climate-controlled and climate-controlled units.</p>



<p>“Few near-term catalysts for a meaningful turnaround in demand are evident, as a historically weak housing market and ongoing development continue to pressure rental rates,” especially in Sun Belt markets that are struggling to absorb excess supply, according to a new national report from Yardi Matrix.</p>



<p>On the positive side, the market has hosted a high level of investment activity in recent months, including Public Storage’s $10.7 billion acquisition of National Storage Affiliates.</p>



<p>Get more insight into U.S. street rate growth trends, new supply and more in the <a href="https://www.yardimatrix.com/publications/download/file/8538-MatrixSelfStorageNationalReport-April2026?signup=false">Yardi Matrix Self Storage National Report for April 2026</a>, which draws from 2,619 self storage properties in various stages of development. Yardi Matrix also maintains operational profiles for 32,803 completed U.S. self storage facilities.</p>
