Nearly 1.3 million units are in lease-up phase, constraining multifamily rent growth for the remainder of 2026, with full-year growth likely to be limited despite declining construction starts. Economic pressures including weakening consumer spending and slowing job growth offset reduced supply, while transaction activity remains muted due to seller pricing expectations.
