Affordable housing starts are decelerating, with fully affordable starts dropping almost 20% year-over-year in Q1 2026 due to rising construction costs, elevated interest rates, labor shortages, and declining tax credit pricing. Deliveries will concentrate in primary metros including Los Angeles, NYC, Austin, Dallas, Miami, San Francisco, and Denver.
